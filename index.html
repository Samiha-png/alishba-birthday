Alishba,

Nineteen looks good on you. I don't say that lightly — I've watched you carry this last year with the same calm you carry everything with, and somehow still find room to take care of everyone around you, including me, especially me.

You have this quiet way of loving people. You don't announce it, you just show up for it — remembering the small things, checking in when no one asked you to, looking after me in ways I don't think you even notice you're doing. I notice. I always notice.

So here's to you, Alishba. To your calm heart and your not-so-calm jealousy, to the way you look after everyone before yourself, to nineteen years of being exactly who you are. Thank you for being in my life. Genuinely — thank you.

Happy Birthday. I hope every good thing finds you this year, and every year after.
